# Crystal Frequency
### Harmonics of Creation

## Overview

By squeezing a [[Mana|mana]] crystal in a rhythmic fashion, a consistent effect can be produced.
By striking it precisely, a sudden and powerful effect can be produced.
These two truths paved the way for many inventions and advancements in [[Humans|human]] technology, and are one of the many reasons that, to this day, it is dangerous to enter the ancient ruins from their fallen empires.

## History

Like most schools of thought in regards to the arcane, [[Ancient Arcana]] was the inciting incident that led to the pursuit of tapping into this power.
The average person could do nothing with the crystals as they were, which meant that that devastating power was only accessible to a very limited population.

It was only a matter of time before a method was found, with how doggedly the research was pursued in those early days of history.
And with the discovery came rapid advancement, the likes of which humanity had never seen!
So much convenience, such a wild shift in quality of life resulted in an explosion of industry
