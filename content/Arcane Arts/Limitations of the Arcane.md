# Limitations of the Arcane
### Restriction Breeds Creativity

## Overview

The limiting factors of the Arcane Arts are an important thing to take note of.
No amount of study or determination have been able to overcome the following:
Teleportation of any sort.
Resurrection of the deceased.
Manipulation of time.
Creating a space within another space.
Long distance communication.
Conjuring food.

The Arcane Arts are not all powerful.
They do not give one the ability to grant wishes.

There is no known way to accomplish these arts without the use of [[Mana|mana]].
Whether internal, environmental, crystalized, or otherwise, [[Mana|mana]] must be available to convert.

## History

Over the many years that humanity has had access to these arts, they have attempted to push the boundaries, to break through into these impossible areas.
