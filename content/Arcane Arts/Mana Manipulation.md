# Mana Manipulation
### Attunement With Nature

## Overview

The study of external, free-floating, unstable [[Mana|mana]] in an environment.
By using what is already available in the area, those who practice this method are capable of achieving things without the ever-present fear experienced by practitioners of [[Ancient Arcana|ancient arcana]].
The effects are far smaller, and more controlled, though the requirements are significantly higher.

## Tools

Practitioners of this style of the arcane arts are ill equipped if they do not possess a heavily researched notebook, a focusing object, and a wide brimmed hat.

The hat is the most important of the groupings, as the hats given to apprentices by their masters are capable of showing the wearer the night sky, simply by looking up through the brim.
No matter where they are, whether underground, in day time, or both, the night sky still shows through.

The focusing object can be anything, so long as it is something that they can devote their primary focus to.
Something they have a close attachment to works best.

## History

This aspect of the arcane arts was created by a young woman who witnessed an explosion of [[Ancient Arcana|ancient arcana]], and came to know the feeling.
And through that feeling, she learned what free floating [[Mana|mana]] feels like.
And from that understanding, a fantastical series of events was set in motion.

Detection led to measurement, measurement led to experiments, and experiments... led to inconsistent results.
It wasn't until many years after, her apprentice was outside and staring up at the stars, pointing out that some had shifted from the previous night.
Goodness knows why this was what spurred the woman on, possibly out of desperation to make the world make any sort of sense.

The night sky was studied, more measurements, more experiments.
There did not seem to be any pattern or reason to the movements of the stars.
There also did not seem to be any association between the night sky and the freely floating mana...
Until there was finally a breakthrough.
A series of stars that had stayed in position, on a day where the measurement of a specific type of [[Mana|mana]] had not changed.
A foothold!
And it was able to be replicated!
