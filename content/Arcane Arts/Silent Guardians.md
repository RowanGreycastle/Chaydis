# Silent Guardians
### Eternal Guards of Fallen Empires

## Overview

Through a more advanced combination of [[Crystal Frequency]] and [[Mana Manipulation]], inanimate objects were given movement, purpose, and direction.
The crystal allows for repetitive, specific movement, while the flow of free [[Mana]] functions as a nigh-infinite power source to allow the objects to continue to move, long after they should have stopped.
These inventions only came about at the height of each human empire, and they are the last things left roaming the abandoned halls of the ancient ruins.
Many are nigh indestructible with modern tools.
