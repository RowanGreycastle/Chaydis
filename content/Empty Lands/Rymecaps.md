# Rymecaps
### Frozen Savior of the Early Varin

## Overview

So named for their perpetually frosty-looking blue and white cap, and the place where they grow best.  
These mushrooms can be found along the coasts of the [[Empty Lands]], growing on the driftwood and drying it out.  
Ocean water acts as a natural anti-fungal, though the main thing that keeps this from growing on ships is the temperature.  
The stalk of the mushroom is thin and hollow inside, like a straw.  
The cap is wide and thin, with dense gills along the underside.  
Each mushroom stores an immense amount of spores that can be released if jostled too heavily.  
The spores themselves aren't dangerous, though if they're inhaled they can cause dizziness...  
Not the safest ailment to endure while balancing on piles of floating wood.  
The mushrooms are edible, according to [[Varin|varin]] sources, and reportedly quite good if fried with sea salt.  
When burnt, they give off a smoke that's close to the scent of the wood they were attached to.
