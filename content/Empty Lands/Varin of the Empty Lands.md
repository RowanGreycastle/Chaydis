# Varin of the Empty Lands
### Warm Together, Chilled Apart

## Overview

Those who live at the edge of the world must rely on one another to survive, sharing beliefs and traditions around the outer ring.
Working together is the only option, and sharing resources is the best way to make sure that everyone makes it to tomorrow.

## Naming Traditions

A [[Varin|varin]]'s last name comes from the name of the village that raised and taught them.

Once a [[Varin|varin]] has learned to speak, they are asked to pick a name for themselves.
There isn't a time limit to consider the choice, but the new arrival is encouraged to go with whatever pops to mind.
Many [[Varin|varin]] names are just fun to say!

## Shared Customs and Values

A Common House is always built within [[Varin|varin]] villages.
Dinners are (when possible) eaten together in the Common House, to strengthen community and cut down on fuel use.
Food brought back from hunts is distributed through the village by this method, ensuring that not even visitors go hungry.

Each evening, after dinner has wrapped up, one of the current village heads tells a tale.
Participation in the story is encouraged, but not required.
Through this method, histories and important information are distributed to all who listen, and stories are repeated to stick in the mind.

Cunning and wit are highly valued, though it is important to be able to laugh at oneself and not take things too seriously.
A smirk is an expression that invites snowballs.

Education is the job of the whole village.
When a new [[Varin|varin]] is born into the world, the new life is seen as a younger sibling in need of good role models.
The teaching of practical skills to the newcomer is highly valued, while practical jokes and pranks are scorned in those early years.

It is best to keep a shadow within arm's reach.
Shadows are safety, and safety allows one to live another day.
The [[Shadow Meld|gift]] given by [[Shax]] ensure that every shadow is another place to hide.

## Hunting Traditions

[[Varin]] are tricksy out of necessity, as they do not grow to be particularly large or strong.
In fact, they don't grow much at all.
Additionally, one of the few sources of meat that isn't from a tamed [[Wooly Beetle]] comes from the [[Redbelly Daggerjaw]].
These fish are so aggressive that they'll partially beach themselves in an attempt to nab a meal from the shore.
The common method for hunting them is to lure them in with one of the hunters as bait, after setting something up to cast enough of a shadow to hide within.
Once the fish is trapped, the hunters strike; a decisive blow enacted at a time of vulnerability, as they couldn't hope to overpower it otherwise.
The one that was bait is granted the title of hunter, and gifted one of the fish's namesake teeth to be fashioned into a proper dagger once it's been brought back to the village.
The knife is less a functional tool, and more of a symbol of the status of hunter.

## Government

Each village has at least two heads; one who demonstrates cunning and guile above others, and one who displays eloquence to a high degree.
The methodology for choosing the two heads differs greatly.

The one who is fluent in the ways of words must be voted into the position, and then must travel to the nearest village to the East and West and meet with their people.
They must be accepted in both neighboring villages.
If they should return, they will be named as a head, and granted the title of Smile.

The one who is adept at trickery must, in order to be considered, participate in a yearly prank competition.
They must win in such a way where their adeptness can not be called into question.
Once this has been done, they must travel to the [[Sunlands]], live there for one year, and make it back.
Once they have returned with a prize from their trip, they will be named as a head and granted the title of Fang.

During times of peace, the Smiles determine much of the common comings and goings of the villages, settling disputes and organizing events.
During times of strife and war, the Fangs come to the forefront to ensure that the village survives whatever hardship comes their way.

[[Vex]] is the only known Fanged Smile.

## Method of Dress

The only material available to the [[Varin|varin]] for creating new clothing was, for many years, the pelt of the [[Wooly Beetle]].
The traditional clothing of the small foxfolk consists of body plates from such a beetle, treated and worked until soft and flexible.
The wooly outer texture worked to insulate against moisture and cold alike, with the wool itself able to repel water.
This, unfortunately, also made it quite difficult to clean the pelts completely, and so a smell always seemed to linger.

Now they're capable of customizing outfits discovered on derelict vessels off their shores to suit their needs.
The [[Sunlands]] lose many things to the [[Outring Ocean]].

## Art and Architecture

The windbreaking walls set up around the villages serve double duty.
They reduce the amount of snow blown over homes, while extending shadows cast away from town.
These started as a necessity, though it eventually grew into a sort of artistic competition to see who could cast the longest shadows using the smallest amount of material.

Most non-stove surfaces in a [[Varin|varin]] [[Stove Homes|stove home]] are covered in woven blankets; the wool is harvested from the [[Wooly Beetle]].
The blankets absorb the heat from the stove, keeping the residents comfortable and warm while they sleep.
Each new resident is expected to add a section to the blanket.

## New Life and Coming of Age Practices

On nights where four [[Shadow Lanterns]] gather in a village, it is known that [[Shax]] will appear before the residents.
He will take the offered shadows to form a [[Varin|varin]] anew!
Their form will be the one that they will have for their life, but their mind will be feral and cunning.
Through demonstration and instruction, the newcomer can be brought to understand their new life.

A name is taken by a newly formed [[Varin|varin]] once they have gained the ability of speech.

A knife is taken by a newly proven [[Varin|varin]] hunter once they have landed their first [[Redbelly Daggerjaw|Redbelly]].

## Common Taboos

"*One does not tamper with food.*
*Food maintains life, and so tampering with it is tampering with someone's life.*
*One does not sabotage a home.*
*A home is safety, and so sabotaging it is sabotaging someone's safety.*
*One does not trick those who cannot yet trick.*
*We all come into this world knowing little; it would be unsporting to spoil the days of learning.*"

## Historical Figures
