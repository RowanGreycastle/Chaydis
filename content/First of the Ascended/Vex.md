# Vex
### First of the Varin

## Overview

The Ascension was a strange event indeed!
Vex came in, knowing full well that this had happened before.
She was the first of her kind to be uplifted, but the [[Varin|varin]] had not come from an unthinking origin, and she had kept everything during the process.
[[Shax]] did not wish to take who she had been away; all of the [[Varin|varin]] were his children.

Meeting the rest of the [[The Six and Three|gods]] in person, and their strange creations, involved a fair amount of restraint on her part
There were several moments where she needed to bite her tongue, lest she find herself being TOO clever.

It was through her action that the [[Empty Lands]] began to look more lively, and through her wisdom that the [[Inuri|inuri]] were convinced of their task.
