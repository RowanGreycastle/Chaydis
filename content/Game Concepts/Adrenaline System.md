# Adrenaline System
### Game Concept

Rough draft first attempt at making a sort of mana system for martial characters.
First, it's meant to give martial characters a resource system to interact with that allows them to do cool abilities.
Second, it's meant to cause physical exertion after the battle, once things have started to calm down.

## Newest Attempt

Players start each combat with 0 out of a maximum of 10 Adrenaline.
Certain abilities grant Adrenaline.
Other abilities require that a certain amount of Adrenaline is already achieved.
No abilities that grant Adrenaline can be used while Adrenaline is at its maximum.

Default Adrenaline actions that can be taken:
Powerful Strike (+1 Adrenaline): Doubles the damage of the attack action.
Quick Step (+1 Adrenaline): Doubles the range of a movement action.
Focus (Requires 1 or more Adrenaline): Reduce Adrenaline by 1.
Fishing Blow (+5 Adrenaline, Requires 5 or more Adrenaline): Deal 10x damage to a single target. Immediately drop to 0 Stamina.

Something available to only some:
Intimidating Shout (+1 Adrenaline): Increase Target Priority by 2 points.

## Original Attempt

Extra effects can be used which increase the Adrenaline Points.
Any Adrenaline Points in excess of 5 cause damage to the Stamina Points, but it hard caps out at 10 Adrenaline Points.
This should hopefully allow for some satisfying effects, as well as allowing for burst options, and 'blaze of glory' attacks that have a 'tired out' effect.
Adrenaline Points reset after ten minutes of rest.

Further thoughts on the idea:
Some actions which increase Adrenaline Points can only be taken once your Adrenaline is already at a baseline before use.
Possible options for a boosted action would be to double the damage of all attacks this turn, or throw in a Trip on the next attack.
Lots of possible modifiers.
Swing for the fences move could be something that's meant to be an all-or-nothing fight ender.
Maybe a whirlwind attack allowing for a strike against every adjacent and diagonal enemy.
Maybe a plunging attack meant to pierce a particularly durable large enemy.
