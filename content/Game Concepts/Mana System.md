# Mana System
### Game Concept

Rough draft first attempt at crafting a mana system for use in the environmental AND Arcane Arts rules.
Initial idea is to have a pool of either coloured tokens, or different coloured D10s that can be set to the value of tokens that would've been set to the associated mana type.
The mana pool is what is available free-floating in the immediate area, and influences the area with certain effects.

## Newest Attempt

Each location has mana, and this mana is represented as a pool arranged into a 6-pointed star.
Each of the points represents one of the types of mana, and the center represents the Neutral state.
The way it's organized around the points is Order > Fluidity > Growth > Freedom > Rigidity > Stagnation.

## Original Attempt

The mana with a counterpart only count the difference between them for determining effects.
So for example, if there is 6 Growth and 3 Stagnation, the environment would count as having a Growth value of 3, and a Stagnation of 0.
If an arcane art calls for 6 Growth to be used, it would be able to be used, as that is a cost, not a passive effect.

Those with internal mana can detect changes in the environment with varying degrees of proficiency with distance.

When mana is used, most of it converts to its opposing type, though there is always a chance that some is lost to the Neutral state.
Each time mana is used, roll 1d6 for each mana involved.
Each result of a 1 is one unit of mana being lost to a Neutral state.

A mana value of 3 in an environment will have a minor impact on the world.
A mana value of 6 will have a more impactful presence.
A mana value of 9 will have a heavy impact that warps the world.
A mana value of 10 will have a catastrophic impact on the world.

Growth 3 leads to plants growing more readily and abundantly, and animals growing up healthy.
Growth 6 leads to crops growing in weeks instead of months, which can become unstable; animal population booms, resulting in possible overpopulation.
Growth 9 leads to crops growing in minutes instead of weeks, which will devour any mana in the soil in the process; animal population explodes, resulting in overpopulation to the point where it spills into other areas.
Growth 10 leads to plants seeking out alternate sources of energy, and animals rapidly adapting to survive within this accelerated life cycle.

A mana value higher than 5 results in the mana gradually forming solidified crystals.
A mana value of 6 will take a week to reduce, but the higher above 5 it is, the quicker the crystals form.

A person's internal mana capacity determines how much they can amplify an environmental mana.
Someone with 3 internal mana can spend up to 3 actions to boost 1 environmental mana to counting as if 3 was being used.
This allows for more mana to escape into the environment, though it, like the used mana, will be swapped to either the opposite type, or to Neutral.

Each mana added to the environment has a 50% chance of causing damage to the Stamina Points.
