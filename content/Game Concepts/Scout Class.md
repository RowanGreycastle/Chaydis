# Scout Class
### Game Concept

Rough draft first attempt at making a fun class idea for scouting characters.
In theory, they wouldn't be a combat class, and instead would be utility and information based.
Initial idea involves them spending the time where others are performing a ten minute rest to Scout Ahead and gather information.
Idea involves the player choosing to go Safe or Risky with the scouting.
Not yet 100% sure on what those two will do, we'll figure it out when we get there.
Scout Ahead gives information on the next encounter, whether it be a trapped hallway, a room with a weird puzzle-looking setup in it, or a combat encounter.
Whatever it is, the players get to know about it before they're even there.
If it's a combat encounter, the scout gets some Sneak Points to spend on messing with the formation of the enemy AFTER everything has been laid out, to represent the tricks and antics they were able to play while they were moving alone.
Some antics could include repositioning enemies, applying debuffs, or placing traps on the map.
They use direct combat as a last resort, but may take a few potshots if they can get away with it.
Their strength does not lie in their combat prowess.
