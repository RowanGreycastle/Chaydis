# Stamina Points
### Game Concept

Rough draft first attempt at making a satisfying hit point system.
First, it allows for a more cartoony sort of fighting system, in that folks can get beaten up, but only really die of the stakes get RAMPED.
Second, it ties the other systems to the hit point system, because it's really more of an exhaustion system.

## Newest Attempt

Players start each day with 10 Stamina (+ or - modifiers from sleeping arrangements).
Performing in town activities costs ~1 Stamina (shopping, research, information gathering, etc.).
Travelling costs 1 Stamina per 30 minutes of travel by foot.
Travelling by cart is free (for reasonable amounts of travel in a day).
Taking a hit in combat reduces your stamina.
Focusing mana reduces your stamina.
Too much Adrenaline reduces your stamina at the end of combat.

10 minutes of rest/downtime activities regains all Stamina, -1 per rest taken before this.
Food + Drink gives +3 to the modifier, and modifiers going forward, once per day.
In character discussions or downtime activities also come with their own boon.
A good night's rest resets the rest counter.

Once a player is out of Stamina Points while not in combat, they become tired but are still able to function.
The tasks they perform take twice as long, unless they are forced to work faster.
If forced, the chances for mistakes increase.

In combat, Stamina Points are the first thing to take damage.
Once a player is out of Stamina Points in combat, they can still fight, and they can still run, but they lose access to most of their special abilities.
The next hit they take will knock them down.
If they take a hit while they're down, they'll be knocked out.

## Original Attempt

I'm going to toss around some numbers for examples, but this could easily be adjusted.
At the beginning of each day, each character gets ~3 freebie 'hits' that won't cause any negative side effects, possibly boosted with armor.
Each character has ~5 'hits' until they're exhausted and can't do anything else.
An attack landing in combat takes one 'hit'.
Doing activities in town takes one 'hit'.
Travelling long distances takes one 'hit'.
The only way to regain them is with rest.

One ten minute rest per day must be done with food and drink to avoid penalties.
Others can involve in-character discussion or downtime activities, all of which come with a boon.
Each 10 minute rest gives all stamina back, as well as +3 bonus, -1 per 10 minute rest taken before that.
A good night's sleep will also refresh all Stamina Points.
Better sleeping accommodations grant bonus Stamina Points.
A proper sleep resets the rest counter.
