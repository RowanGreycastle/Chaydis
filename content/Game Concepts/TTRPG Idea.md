# TTRPG Idea
### Game Concept

The basic idea that I'm running with is more of a resource based system, less of a luck based one.
My intent is for the Arcane Arts to be relatively open to experimentation with some signpost abilities, and for the same to be true of the Martial Arts.
Having a few key resource pools for each class type, and different ways to interact with those resource pools seems like a solid way to do things.
Planning on using the 3 action economy of Pathfinder 2nd edition and Slay the Spire.
Possible deck of cards for the RNG element?
Don't want to have TOO many different elements.
Explore the idea of combat taking place on a flower-shaped Hex Grid, with multiple units capable of being on the same tile, and the grid representing an area of combat as opposed to a 5-foot square.
The center of the grid is focused around whichever person is carrying the light source.

Maintain low health.
Have damage numbers be fairly low for most abilities/attacks.
Have attacks be consistent, but actively able to be dodged.
Have monsters move on their turn, and then telegraph what their next attack is going to be.
Include an aggro system that shifts where the monsters are going to attack, with alternate goals having a specific aggro threshold that needs to be bypassed to have an effect.

Create a variety of basic creature types, melee, ranged, utility, and DANGEROUS.
Create variations of these, to give the players something to discover as they work.
Throw in speedy, bulky, stealthy, tricksy, and a few other modifiers.

For 'XP', current idea is to use large impacts on dungeon ecosystems as a milestone.
Going from level 1 to level 2 requires 1 major change.
2 to 3 requires two.
So on and so forth.
Levelling up gives access to perk selections, possibly one, possibly multiple, not yet certain.
Need to inbuild overall weakness into base characters, so that at level 1, whatever perks they decide to put things into feel meaningful.
