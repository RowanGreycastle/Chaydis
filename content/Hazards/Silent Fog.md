# Silent Fog
### Humid, Clingy, and Deadly

## Overview

A dense fog found within the island chains of [[Weva]].
Within, healing of all sorts ceases.
Minor nicks and scrapes refuse to close.
Any sounds from within the mists are deadened, masking cries for help.
Additionally, this makes it a prime hunting ground for insects.
The normal telltale sounds of buzzing and chirping are completely absent, only the sensation of crawling and biting remains.
