# Kolken
### Ancestor of Buken

## Appearance

A fluffy, snake-like creature that is quite adept at climbing and coiling.
Unlike their meat eating cousins, the kolken do not possess fangs, or even teeth in general.
Instead, they have a set of horns atop their head to defend their territory, and a club-like tail that is used to smash open fruit from the [[Kolkol Tree|kolkol tree]].

## Location

These creatures were found almost exclusively atop the [[Kolkol Tree|kolkol tree]].
The high vantage point allowed them awareness of, and protection from, most predators of their species.
The close proximity to food for their specific diets made this a perfect place to nest.

## Significance

The kolken was identified in ages past as a being capable of altering one's luck, as [[Humans|humans]] had witnessed the snake catching dangerous fruits before they could come crashing down.
Disturbing them was seen as taboo, and would guarantee any who did so an unfortunate life.

## Diet

Kolken are frugivores, primarily subsisting off of the fruit of the [[Kolkol Tree|kolkol tree]], though they won't shy away from other sorts.
Kolken have never been seen consuming anything other than fruit.

## Relationships

The kolken lived in symbiosis with the [[Kolkol Tree|kolkol tree]], fending off pests in exchange for safety and food.

## Descendants

After the separation of the [[Continents|continents]], the favored environment of the [[Kolkol Tree|kolkol tree]] no longer truly existed.
The tree went through different developments, and so did the toothless snake that had made them its home for so long.

Those who wound up on the continent of [[Laef]] were slower to adapt, being periodically driven from atop the tree by the shifting leaf properties.
Over time, the kolken of this area developed antlers that formed an almost cup shape, allowing them to move multiple eggs, or a whole kolkol fruit about with them.

Over on the continent of [[Sten]], the kolken weren't anywhere near as lucky.
With no high tree to keep them aware and safe, they were easy pickings until they determined they could hide within the 'branches' of the now stubby [[Kolkol Tree|kolkol tree]].
Antlers became less useful, as they would just catch on the dense cover.
The smaller fruit didn't require clubbing, though the ground level made things significantly more dangerous, and the tail was repurposed for defense.

Kolken that found themselves separated from the [[Kolkol Tree|kolkol tree]] completely who were captured and cared for by the [[Humans|humans]] of [[Weva]] found themselves utterly dependent upon the these caretakers.
A more sedentary lifestyle led to smaller horns, weaker muscles, a wider body, and an inability to move quickly.
Like their [[Sten]] cousins, they no longer needed to club their food.
Unlike them, they also did not need to club anything else, and so the muscles for raising their tail atrophied.
Now known as Buken, the kolken of [[Weva]] are kept as pets and livestock, valued for their egg production and their docile, dopey nature.
