# Kolkol Tree
### Historical Separator of Domains

## Location

These exceptionally tall trees grew on the border between the domains of [[Deepest Jungle]] and [[Highest Mountain]].
The stony ground found here wasn't suitable for the growth of most types of plants, but specialized roots allowed this tree to delve deep to get the nutrients it required.

## Significance

When returning from the jungle, the sight of these trees brought relief to travelers, and so they came to be seen as a good luck charm.
Standing directly beneath one is seen as one challenging fate.
If not for the fruit that these trees produce, it's possible that [[Humans|humans]] would not have survived those early days.

## Produce

The kolkol tree produces kolkol fruit, which is egg-shaped, and roughly the size of a watermelon.
The large seeds of the fruit are arranged in a layout similar to a pinecone within the flesh.
The flesh is fairly hard, though the insides are soft and squishy (other than the seeds, of course).
The scent of the innards is similar to that of a sweet melon, though the flavor is a muted sweetness.

## Relationships

The [[Kolken|kolken]] lived in symbiosis with the tree, fending off pests from its leafy canopy, but taking some of the fruit before it falls as payment.

## Descendants

After the separation of the [[Continents|continents]], the favored environment of the kolkol tree no longer truly existed.

Those that somehow wound up on the continent of [[Laef]] died out almost completely, though eventually a parasitic adaptation developed, with the help of the [[Kolken|kolken]].
Lacking other kolkol trees to travel to, [[Kolken|kolken]] would find themselves stranded on the tops of other trees with kolkol seeds.
Some of these seeds wound wind up in suitable positions to sprout, and grow roots down into the tree they'd been set upon, with the body of the tree growing down, following the roots, instead of up!
Possibly a strange side effect of the uniform heights of [[Laef]]'s tree line.
An additional side effect: the leaves at the top of the tree are only a comfortable place for a nest during non-fruiting seasons.
As the weather becomes more appropriate for the fruit to grow, the leafy top creates a pollen meant to irritate, encouraging any creatures with their fruits to migrate elsewhere.

The ones that were left on [[Sten]] had a similar rapid adaptation, no doubt prompted by the strange new environment.
Rocky mountains and deep valleys meant that more energy had to be stored in a single seed to have roots reach down far enough to get the required nutrients to survive.
Single seeds meant smaller fruits.
Larger roots meant shorter trunks.
The kolkol trees here now look FAR more like bushes; short stubby things with dense foliage.
This species is quite stubborn, and nearly impossible to truly get rid of, as the absorption of anything available has resulted in these 'trees' infusing themselves with various metals.
