# Day of Arrival
### Celebration of Life

## Overview

Once the frigid Winter and harsh Summer months have passed, the weather becomes much more manageable.
Springtime is the best time for gatherings, for harvests, and to congregate to celebrate any life that has come into this world.

One could view it as a birthday for everyone, where gifts are exchanged, a festival of sorts is held, banquets are had, and blossoming love is encouraged!
The day's events are customarily led by one of the youngest in attendance who is capable of speaking; with prompting from their parents, of course.
During the event, it is expected that they dress in a baggy, hooded travelling robe; the ceremonial garb of [[Verath]].

The activities of the day generally focus on frivolous fun activities for the amusement of the younger folk, while eyes are kept out for any young [[Kalis|kalis]] that seems to have retained their memories from a previous life.
It's uncommon, but not unheard of.
