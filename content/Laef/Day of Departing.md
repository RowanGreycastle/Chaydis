# Day of Departing
### Celebration of Those Who Lived

## Overview

As the months grow colder, and the leaves begin to fall from the trees with the approach of Winter, [[Kalis|kalis]] communities gather in a ritualistic sendoff of those who did not make it through the year.
A fond farewell is given, so as to show the deceased how much they meant to those that were left behind.

One could see it as a group funeral, where everyone gathers together to celebrate the lives of those no longer here.
It's customary for the mayor or an elder to dress up in ceremonial garb, to emulate [[Verath]] and assist in guiding the lost back to their creator.

The bodies of the deceased leave behind their furs to be kept safe until the Day of Arrival.
It is crucial that the bodies of the deceased are recovered for this purpose.
