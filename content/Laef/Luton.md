# Luton
### The Town of Fresh Starts

## Overview

This site was originally a single road house situated in the fork of the Lilac River.
Over time, it developed into a proper settlement.
The inhabitants were [[Kalis|kalis]] and [[Humans|humans]], and that's stayed fairly consistent ever since.

Located on the Eastern shore of [[Laef]], it's only a short travel across the [[Crossed Seas]] to travel from the [[Unclaimed Continent]], and so it should be no wonder that the human population is as large as it is here.
This puts it squarely within the [[Sunlight Zone]], making daytime activities quite safe.
All the same, gatehouses overlook the roads in and out, a ten foot tall encircles the town.
Bramble bushes grow up the outside of the wall, and watch towers stand tall at each corner.
