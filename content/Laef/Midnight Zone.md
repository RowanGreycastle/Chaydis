# Midnight Zone
### Edge of the Abyss

## Overview

No light is capable of making it through the canopy.
The only source of brightness in this far either comes from the [[Twilight Zone]]'s residual, or from those passing through.
The creatures here are still more difficult to see, due to their natural camouflage adaptations.

Hunting here is done without sight, though the creatures are still capable of sensing light.
The creatures down here are few and far between, as the plant life has needed to adapt to this light-poor environment in order to survive.
Alternate food sources are used.
