# Newpelt
### New Life Celebration

## Overview

When a [[Kalis|kalis]] is born without black, white, or grey colouring their pelt, a celebration is thrown to invite this 'new life' into the cycle.
This stems from the belief that their pelt colours indicate the way that their previous life ended.	Newpelt generally falls on the same day as the Day of Arrival, or a few days after if the colouration is unclear.
This tends to be when the good alcohol is brought out of storage.
