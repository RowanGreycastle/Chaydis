# Seeking Shelter
### A Board Game of Laef

## Overview

A competitive board game with a theme of searching for a good burrow spot with no danger.
The board has one less 'good spot's than there are players, ensuring that there will always be an odd one out.
The objective is to discover a 'good spot' without being tricked into choosing a 'bad spot', and not being the last one without a 'good spot'.

The game can handle between two and six players.
The tokens on the board are arranged in a grid pattern, with five tokens per player.
The amount of 'good spot' tokens is equal to one less than the total number of players.
The rest are 'bad spot' tokens.

A player may only interact with a single token on their turn.
The token may be Peeked, or Flipped.
To Peek a token, the player is allowed to look at the token to see what hides underneath.
To Flip a token, one commits to taking whatever is on the other side.
The action must be declared aloud before the token is touched.

If a 'good spot' token is flipped, that player has claimed one of the winning positions.
If a 'bad spot' token is flipped, the player who flipped it is the one who has lost, and the game ends.
