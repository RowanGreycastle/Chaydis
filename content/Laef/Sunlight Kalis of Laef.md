# Sunlight Kalis of Laef
### Friendly Gossips

## Overview

The [[Kalis|kalis]] of the outer edge of the continent of [[Laef]] are exceptionally social creatures.
They form bonds based on burrow sharing first, proximity second, and all else third.
Unlike the ones who opt to live deeper in, these [[Kalis|kalis]] stick to safety and security, frequently working together with [[Humans|humans]] to bolster the numbers without risking any mishaps.

## Naming Traditions

A [[Kalis|kalis]]'s last name comes from the name of the burrow that they currently reside in.
One who lacks a last name is to be considered homeless

 The first name is granted by the parents upon their first [[Day of Arrival]].

## Shared Customs and Values

The burrow is not the place for fights or tomfoolery.
Animosity between those in the burrow should be worked out through discussion.
Failure to do so results in one or both parties being evicted to maintain the peace.

Due to the nature of [[Sight Unseen|phantoms]], they are taught to identify common and uncommon symbols and motifs.
Being able to read body language, pick up on intent, and obscure one's own intent are also valuable skills passed along, though it's rare that a tutor would be skilled in every aspect of this.
Without at least some of these skills, it is more likely than not that a young [[Kalis|kalis]] could be tricked by the honeyed words of a field of flowers, only to suffer a lethal bite from the snakes that inhabit it.

## Common Etiquette Rules

Poetry and books are seen as proper gifting choices.
Selections that show insight into the character of the receiver are valued highly, though one must be careful to not inadvertently imply unsavory things.

## Government

Each town has a mayor and a council.
The mayor is an elected official who is allowed to make decisions for the good of the town.
The council is a group of elder [[Kalis|kalis]] who ensure that traditions are not overlooked while these decisions are being made.
