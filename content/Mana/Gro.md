# Gro
### Green Mana

## Overview

Life associated [[Mana|mana]] that holds sway over growth.
There are three main schools of thought in the usage of this [[Mana|mana]]:
Plant growth, gigantification, and tranquility.

The growth of plants may seem quite limited, until one remembers the abundance of strange plant life found within [[Laef]]'s deep woods.

Gigantification, while quite potent, is limited to usage on others; using one's own [[Mana|mana]] on oneself rarely leads to safe results.
Feedback loops are quite detrimental to one's health.

Tranquility may seem like an odd one, though 'growth' was never necessarily physical.
By growing a target spiritually, they become more at peace with the world around them, allowing conflict avoidance more often than not.
It doesn't last forever, and the effects of it can be interrupted by continued acts of hostility.
