# Haizesh
### Mana of the Sky

## Overview

Frequently represented as a golden yellow or a pale blue.
Air associated [[Mana|mana]] that holds sway over the sky.
The main schools of thought in the usage of this [[Mana|mana]] are:
Wind manipulation, impediment removal, and creativity.

The manipulation of wind is a frequently subtle thing, filling the sails of a ship or causing them to flag; blowing the papers off a desk, that sort of thing.
By no means does it need to stay subtle.
Should the need arise, those same gentle breezes can be whipped up into an uncontrollable storm!
Emphasis on uncontrollable.
It is easy to start such things, much more difficult to stop them.

Impediment removal, freedom in essence, is all about the liberating of one from things that might hold them back.
From the removal of chains, ropes, or stockades, to the clearing of a thorny path, to the curing of a nasty toe fungus, freedom shall be found!
It is best to not use this sort of [[Mana|mana]] for curing impeding diseases too frequently, or one might discover themselves growing steadily shorter.

Creativity is represented here, in the sense that one can use clouds to shape and create the things that they need in the moment that they need them.
A cauldron to mix things in?
A rope to climb down?
A wall to block passage?
It functions for all of these things and more!
Though the construction is never quite as sturdy as it might appear, and it does dissipate over time.
