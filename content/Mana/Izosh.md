# Izosh
### Mana Without Life

## Overview

Frequently represented as white.
Frost associated [[Mana|mana]] that holds sway over freezing things.
The main schools of thought in the usage of this [[Mana|mana]] are:
Temperature manipulation, entry denial, and sense deadening.

Control over the temperature, in this case, is only toward the negative.
By focusing the ability in a very localized way, snow and ice can be formed in seconds.
A more wide spread approach takes time to take effect, and the source isn't subtle, considering steam rises from the one using it.

Being able to prevent entry to an area makes this [[Mana|mana]] exceptionally powerful in regards to defending an area.
Once a barrier has been erected, it takes far longer to take it down again.

Robbing someone of their senses, be it smell, sight, taste, or touch, is not something that should be done lightly.
Those lost senses must be stored somewhere, after all, and the experience can be quite jarring.

## Overload Effects

If an environment is overloaded with this [[Mana|mana]], all movement in the area slows to a crawl, assuming that it doesn't outright stop.
At the edge of the world, further out into the [[Empty Lands]], the snow hangs in the air, unmoving.
Any who are foolish enough to travel out that way find themselves trapped, unaware even that they are trapped.
