# Lurahn
### Mana of the Land

Frequently represented as grey or brown.
Stone associated [[Mana|mana]] that holds sway over the solidity of things.
The main schools of thought in usage of this [[Mana|mana]] are:
Control of rock and metal,  physical and mental defenses, and barrier creation.

## Physical Form

The physical form of this [[Mana|mana]] is all things rock and metal.
The more power that was used to condense it down into something solid, the more solid the end product.

## Environmental Effects

An environment with only a small sway toward this element has hard ground that can be difficult to till.
One with a moderate sway experiences frequently rocky ground, requiring special tools to work.
One with a high sway has that hardness extending to more than just the ground, influencing the trees, plants, and animals around it. The air there can be quite difficult to breathe.
One with an extreme sway has that same hardness extend to the very air, making it nigh impossible for creatures to travel there.

## Historical Events

During experiments performed at the location now known as [[The Cavity]], a large amount of Lurahn [[Mana|mana]] was released from the ground at once, resulting in the air solidifying in an instant.
The occupants of that area were long dead by the time that the concentration dissipated enough to allow them to breathe again.
