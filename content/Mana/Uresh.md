# Uresh
### Mana of the Ocean

## Overview

Frequently represented as a deep blue or dark purple.
Water associated [[Mana|mana]] that holds sway over fluid.
The main schools of thought in the usage of this [[Mana|mana]] are:
Water manipulation, poison creation/destruction, and transformation.

Control over water is a powerful ability indeed!
Whipping it around at speed, syphoning it from the air, pushing things from a good distance away, and many other uses.
It doesn't work well with water that's been contaminated with other things.

Poison is, of course, relative.
In most cases, that would be true.
In the case of these created poisons, all that's required is a living body, and an open wound.
This sort of ability is very dangerous, even to the one creating it.

Being able to change shape into other creatures or beasts allows for so many different opportunities!
To live as someone else, and discover what the world would be like.
To fight and eat as a bear, and experience the world through their eyes.
As with all such things, usage on oneself is ill advised.
