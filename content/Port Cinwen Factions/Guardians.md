# Guardians
### Held the Line

## Overview

The extended family that moved to this land nearly a century ago.  
They took up the task of keeping the horrible night creatures at bay, though only a few knew how to hold a weapon.  
Other tactics were devised in order to lure the creatures out of the port, as that tended to be the safer option for the majority.  
The couple that moved in roughly four decades after, Finn Bresmaras and his wife, proved invaluable for teaching archery and trapmaking.  
Unfortunately, the hunter was caught out one night; his body was never found.

The family, to this current day, continue to practice what was learned from the hunter, the use of bow, knife, spear, and trap.  
Allowing those skills to get rusty would mean that they could not be passed on, and that would in turn tarnish the hunter's legacy.

In more recent times, there's significantly less for the Night Guardians to keep watch for, which occasionally calls their lack of participation in fishing into question.  
Some sons and daughters have started to take up the local trade, while others insist that it's important to have them around in case something happens.  
Others still have managed to slip by relatively unnoticed, getting away with doing very little.  
And further others have managed to bring their family comfort through subtle trade deals with other ports.
