# Historians
### Searching for Ancient Ruins

## Overview

An established town on the Northernmost tip of the [[Unclaimed Continent]] was quite the interesting location for one studying history.
The sudden dip of the coast, showing the outline of where [[The Moon|the moon]] once sat.

There are signs abound of underground structures found here and there, but they've yet to find a genuine entrance.
One day, gods willing, they hope to discover a way inside to get a better idea of what secrets might be found from before [[The Moon|the moon]] was lifted.

It's been slow going thusfar, but they've only arrived recently.
Surely there will be a breakthrough soon!
It would come much more quickly if they were trusted by the community.
