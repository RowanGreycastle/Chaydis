# Intelligencia
### Pushing the Port to Greater Heights

## Overview

Most having only arrived recently in [[Port Cinwen]], these folks are mainly here due to the constant throughfare of people from far off lands.  
They view the pursuit of knowledge as a noble goal, and believe that their pursuit (real or fabricated) makes them stand above the rest.  
There's no doubting that some of them certainly know their stuff, and have helped the residents of the port with improving there lives in a few ways...  
But considering the amount of boasting that comes along with it, it's debatable as to whether it's worth it to have them around.

Counted amongst their number, a woman classically trained in an ancient human art of fighting.  
She and her family arrived many years before the others, though unfortunately not early enough to be of any help against the creatures of the night.
