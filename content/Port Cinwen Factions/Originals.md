# Originals
### The Families Who Started It All

## Overview

The fishermen and their families who descended from the founders of the town.
Their association is through long-standing relationships between relatives.
Some have remained positive over the years.
Others have soured.
Even so, in times of crisis, there is an urge to band together with one another against all else.

These families know well what is hidden beneath the storehouse the families all use, and they're quite tight lipped about it.
