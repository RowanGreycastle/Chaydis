# Almine
### Swirling Sands Ever Dancing

## Overview

A human from the [[Black Desert]].
Back when they were she, she went searching in the desert for something lost, and wound up lost herself.
Wandering in the cold, with no idea which direction was which, she called out for help, and risked the attention of the beasts beneath the sands.
The sands began to move, shifting and swirling around her, until a spirit made itself known.
An offer was given; an offer was accepted.
And two became one.

In their time in the desert, they met Jek, the [[Inuri|inuri]], and when the opportunity arose for a life elsewhere, they took Jek to this little trade port.
Hopefully their [[Inuri|inuri]] friend will attract enough attention to keep anyone from questioning them directly about strange goings on near their home.
The more people here, the easier it will be for them to live peacefully without having to explain anything... awkward.

## Information

Almine and Swirling Sands Ever Dancing are [[Spirit Bonded|spirit bonded]], though they have only been for a short while.
Their body is that of a 27 year old human, and they have been travelling together for 3 years.
They present as androgynous, hiding themselves beneath flowing cloaks and behind wooden masks.
