# Eydis Everstone
### Seer of Spirits

## Overview

Spirit Guide of the port.
Eydis dresses in outfits that obscure their form, and has done for quite a while.
They've been with the port since shortly after the [[Acita|acita]] arrived, at which point they were a child.
Ever since, they've felt a responsibility toward handling the things others can't see.
Through their hard work, the [[Spirits|spirits]] that come to visit (which most of the adults can't see, and hardly believe exist) don't get too terrible an impression of the place.
It's because of this that they've come to learn many secrets that they shouldn't have any possible way of knowing, and came to inadvertently blurt out an embarrassing fact about the potentially late husband of a certain woman while drunk...
