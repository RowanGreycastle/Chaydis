# Fern Honeyblood
### The Soul of Cinwen

## Overview

Living very much 'in the thick of it' in the port, Fern has a fairly good idea of the goings on behind the scenes.
She knows of the pompous folks from far off lands, and their general dislike of the inuri.
She's familiar with the children's general reverence toward the acita.
She's seen all sorts come and go over the years, her partner in relatively recent years included among them.
However, she's never quite seen anything like the naked hatred Finn and Hester Bagmarsh displayed toward that poor woman looking after all those children around a month ago.
She's made it quite clear what she thinks of the whole thing, and that it's driven a wedge right down the middle of the folks whose families settled this place first.

## Information

Fern is a 68 year old human who treats the village children as her own grandchildren, and makes sure they learn good, honest skills that will help them later in life.
If she needs to recruit someone else in the village to make sure they get the best information, she does it.
