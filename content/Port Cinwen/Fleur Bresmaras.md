# Fleur Bresmaras
### Potential Widow of the Missing Hunter

## Overview

Wife to the late hunter, Finn Bresmaras.  
Having travelled far, and experienced much, she had grown confident that no matter what came, they would find a way through together.  
Their time in Port Cinwen had been comfortable, comparatively.  
It was only recently that Finn had started going out at night again to make sure things were safe.  
He's been gone for nearly two whole months by this point, with no sign of him, but she's been holding out hope that he'll come back eventually, like he always used to.
