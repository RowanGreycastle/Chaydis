# Irees Kelbot
### Caretaker of Children

## Overview

After being allowed to travel on a sailing vessel with the six orphaned children in her care, she hoped to bring them to a safer, more bountiful shore.
Upon arriving, she has found that the locals are a rude and defensive sort, but despite the words they've hurled her direction, they haven't driven her or the children off yet.

She knows full well that she wasn't brought along out of the goodness of anyone's heart, and is simply here to increase numbers and make it harder for the locals to get their way after a few years.
Their anger makes sense to her, but she's not about to take the orphans back to that dangerous place they came from.
