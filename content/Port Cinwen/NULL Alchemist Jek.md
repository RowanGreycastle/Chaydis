# NULL Alchemist Jek
### Lost Inuri of a Broken Colony

## Overview

Following the traditions of the [[Inuri|inuri]], Jek's first name is the name of his queen.
His colony fell, and so he is alone.

His second name is his profession, a chemist who has dealt with poisons and acids aplenty, in a search for many strange and wonderous things for his queen.

His third and final name... is the name that he picked for himself upon finding himself alone in a strange land.
Jek needed the name to introduce himself to his new friend, though he's still figuring the rest of himself out, and is in the process of developing.
