# Odel Bagmarsh
### Compassionate Young Man

## Overview

Odel believes as the other fishermen families do, that this land was claimed and cultivated by them long before it was safe, and that it should not be taken over by people wanting to expand the area and bring in even more people.
He believes that these things can be talked out, and that by discussing with the new arrivals, an understanding can be reached.
He also believes that his parents may have ruined that opportunity with their open and hateful speech regarding a single woman tasked with taking care of six young children.
The disdain he feels for his father after the incident is strong enough that he had to move out on his own, and if ever the two see one another, it quickly devolves into a heated argument that's likely to come to blows.
