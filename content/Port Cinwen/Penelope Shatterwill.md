# Penelope Shatterwill
### Ancient and Determined

## Overview

In small [[Port Cinwen]], Penelope Shatterwill arrived along the [[Sunroads]] with her wife and children.
They moved to this land in the hopes that it would further her wife's connections with the outside world.
She knew full well that her partner was the bright sort, who could turn this little place into a bustling trade port like the cities of old!
And how might she have known that?
Well... put simply, Penelope had been present at the time, thousands of years ago.
Being one who had bonded with a spirit, she had stopped aging long ago; she'd been a part of many family's lives since then, though none were quite as special as her most recent.
Not that she could remember, anyway.

Where her wife sparkled and shone in the realm of academia, Penelope was more one to allow her fists to do the talking.
And those fists knew some mighty powerful words!
Unlike many who travelled here, their small group didn't have to take as many side routes...

Within Penelope, there is, of course, Penelope.
Like many [[Spirit Bonded]] though, there is another.
Her other partner, known as [[Shattered Will On Broken Rocks]], is a vengeful [[Spirits|spirit]], and is quite reactionary.
Due to this presence in her mind, Penelope seems like a completely different person when violence truly overtakes her.
In that she IS another person during those times.
