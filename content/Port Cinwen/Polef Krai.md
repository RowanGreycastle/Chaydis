# Polef Krai
### Old Guardian

## Overview

An old man, and head of the Krai household.  
Polef and his wife and children had travelled up through the Unclaimed Continent, looking for a place to call home.  
When they'd finally arrived here at Port Cinwen, they'd gotten the lay of the land, and knew they needed to be able to call this place home.  
In exchange for protection, the other families offered some of their fish.  
He was happy to agree to such an offer, and all these years later, that is still the calling to which his family answers.  
Certainly, the rune wall has made their training less necessary, but who knows when such a thing might break?

## Information

Polef is an 86 year old human, still capable of standing and moving about on his own, but no longer capable of defending the town, or even himself if something unexpected were to happen.
