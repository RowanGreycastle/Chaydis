# Rayfus
### Newly Arrived Lagos

## Overview

A relatively new arrival to the port.
They've come to this land in search of mysteries to uncover, and truths to discover.
This is only the most recent spot they've stopped on their journey, and the underground ruins have firmly snagged their interest.
Through sheer determination, and working together with their associates, they intend on determining the true nature of the ruins, good or ill.
They've seen the outbursts between [[Odel Bagmarsh]] and his father, and due to the differences in culture, has come to the conclusion that he intends to challenge his father for the position of head of the family.
Such a thing would be a show of complete disrespect among the lagos, and so Rayfus is wary of him.
