# Twang
### String Player of the Quartet

## Overview

One of the four [[Acita|acita]] that descended from [[The Moon|the moon]] to erect the rune barrier that protects [[Port Cinwen]].
When asked about 'why' they did it, Twang is always quick to respond by explaining that it came to them in a dream.
If asked for more information than that, he chooses his words carefully.

Tallest among the quartet, this strange mothfolk wears the flowing garb common to his kind, and prefers yellow and red garments.
Bangles and baubles are of high importance, as is stringed instruments that make a lovely reverberation when strummed.
