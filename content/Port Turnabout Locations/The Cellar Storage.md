# The Cellar Storage
### Storage for the Port

## Overview

Behind the bar of [[The Public House|the public house]], a set of stairs is tucked away beneath a trap door.
The stairs lead down to a small storage room, filled to the brim with the distillery of the various fruit wines and [[Phenge|phenge]].
A few barrels of pickled vegetables, a few hanging dried meats, and nowhere near enough resources for the little port's residents to survive the Winter.
The residents of [[Port Turnabout]] know of this underground storage.

Very few know what's hidden under the shabby looking rug that covers the floor under a few of those barrels.

If one were to move them aside and shuffle over the rug, they'd find another trap door, though this one seems... strange.
It couldn't have been something created by the residents of this little port.
Perfectly circular, a dull brass colour, and with a handle at either side of it.
The covering looks far more sturdy than the rest of the village combined!
And though it's quite heavy, the hinge allows for relatively easy lifting.
It's not locked in any way.

Opening it reveals darkness below, and a ladder that can safely take one down, down, down...
The ladder itself is made of something more metallic, shining with the passage of torchlight.
The walls of the passage appear to be carved from some singular, enormous rockface.
No signs of wear, of cracks, of chips; just a solid surface, the whole way down.
