# The Docks
### Open Air Shrine

## Overview

The crisp ocean air blows up to the Southern gate of [[Port Turnabout]].
A few small docks dot a rocky shore, devoid of driftwood and debris.
The waves flow past in their eternal, cyclic motion.

A single large stone juts from the ground, nearly fifteen feet high and six feet wide.
Carved into the stone, the visage of a woman with cold eyes and a false smile.
How had the finer details of the carving managed to remain, when the stone surrounding it had been weathered and beaten by hundreds of years of wear and tear?
Many offerings sit within the stone bowl at the foot of the carving, ranging from food, to small crafted objects, to even a knife.
Even the freshest offerings are quickly coated in a layer of salt.

Not far from the stone, a small hut stands.
The door is locked, the windows shuttered, and no light comes from within.
The fishing vessels moored to the docks are primarily small, catching the daily meals.
The two largest of the bunch, the [[The Riddius Family|Riddius]] and the [[The Phyrus Family|Phyrus]], are docked quite close to one another.
