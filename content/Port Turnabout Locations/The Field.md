# The Field
### Strange Statues and Perfect Produce

## Overview

Out beyond the Northern gate, across a stretch of desolate, stony field that continues on for a good ways, the one singular field that supports this fishing village can be found.
The field itself is moderately sized, fenced in by a short cobblestone wall; half a person in height at most.

The 'gate' that leads into the oddly green paradise has twin sculptures forming an arch with their hands.
Someone took care to capture very specific details of the duo, who come across as motherly looking women with flowers in their hair.
The eyes of the statue possess a look of affectionate insight.

Beyond the gate, in the fields proper, many a hearty vegetable can be seen growing within, utterly unaware of the inhospitable environment around it.
The walls are much too short to block the wind, and yet one who stands within feels no breeze.
The sunlight barely warms the land here, and yet somehow the land within is warm and bright.
