# The Guard House
### Arms and Storage

## Overview

A short, squat stone building near the Northern gate of [[Port Turnabout]].
A single metal reinforced wooden door blocks the entrance, with a shuttered window not too far from it.
Within, a wooden floor supports an old table and two chairs, upon which a small board game sits.
Along the walls, racks for unused equipment store standard militia faire, with shields and helmets, leathers and spears.
Beyond the table, a trapdoor can be spotted closer to the far wall.

The majority of the day, this place is quite empty.
Whichever two militiamen are on shift are generally out patrolling, though mainly they offer assistance to the residents of the town as able bodied people.
During the night, two keep to the guard house and keep an ear out for trouble, while two more keep eyes on the closed Northern gates and beyond, swapping at regular times.

Down a brief ladder tucked away under the trapdoor, a basement used for storage can be found.
All sorts of equipment that one would want to keep on hand, but wouldn't need most days, is stored away down here.
Spare pots and pans, large scraps of leather, extra helmets, shields, and spears beyond those stored above, chairs, cupboard doors...
If anyone new should happen to come into town and need to make themselves at home, they'd find plenty of useful things for settling in.
