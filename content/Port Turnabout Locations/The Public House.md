# The Public House
### Warm Welcomes in the Cold Wasteland

## Overview

A relatively large stone building near the Northern gate of [[Port Turnabout]].
A duo of solid, metal reinforced wooden doors are located at the top of a short flight of stone steps.

Beyond them, the atmosphere is warm and inviting.
The scent of fish is inescapable in a fishing village, though it's significantly more pleasant here; garlic and fresh produce do wonders to meld with the smell and form something significantly more palatable.
Mats and rugs cover most of the floor, though the bare stone is revealed closer to the fireplace at the center of the building.

To the right of the front door, the front counter and entrance to the small kitchen can be found, with [[Gertrude]] smiling that joyful smile of hers.
The stocky woman with grey-brown hair and sparkling hazel eyes stands at the ready to greet any fisherman or field worker who's come in for a bite to eat, not that she'd turn away other hungry folk.
She gestures to a small listing of various fruit wines and juices, before mentioning the meal of the day.

The recipes she's got have been handed down from her dad, who used to run the place, and each one's guaranteed to warm the belly and the soul.
It ain't some fancy establishment, mind, so she's not about to go preparing something custom for someone picky outside of special circumstances.
