# Gertrude
### Owner of the Public House of Port Turnabout

## Overview

A solidly built woman in her late 40's.
At 5'6", one might not think she'd be able to seem imposing if need be.
If one were to test that, one would discover that the assumption is incorrect.

## Role Within Port Turnabout

Gertrude considers herself a lynchpin of the community, and strives to make sure that she fulfills her role well.
She puts care and effort into her cooking, preparing meals for the occupants of the port so that everyone else can focus on their daily tasks without worrying about such things.
She's got a close relationship with the town militia, as her position allows her to keep an eye on any travelers coming this far out into the middle of the tail end of this unwanted stretch of land.

She's got her suspicions of the motives of the [[The Fyrehart Clan|Fyrehart Clan]], and believes that all of their talk about nobility and such is just to mask the possibility of a grab for power if others let them.
Just like they'd tried in the past.

## Viewed by Others in Port Turnabout

Due to her self assigned role, she tends to keep tabs on the other inhabitants as well.
Very few folks here know more about their neighbors than Gertrude does.
Some of the younger folks view her as a busybody, and actively try to keep things from her.
