# The Fieldworkers
### For Those Who Cannot Sail

## Members

Big Jeff - Years ago, this giant of a man was one of the main folks aboard the [[The Riddius Family|Riddius]] who would be in charge of hauling the fish up, alongside his partner. Ever since the accident, he hasn't been able to so much as look at a boat. If not for Rudy, he'd no doubt still be holed up in [[Gertrude]]'s pub, drowning in drink.

Fray - Her parents were lost in a storm when she was quite young. [[The Fyrehart Clan]] took her in, put a roof over her head, and made sure she knew she never had to pay it back. As soon as she was old enough to work, she was finding odd jobs around town, until eventually she settled on this one, and it seemed to stick.

Rudy - An old man who'd been working these fields nearly by himself for the last long while, only able to recruit help every now and again from the youngsters while their parents were off fishing. He'd been able to get a bit more help after drawing Fray's attention his way, through his connection to [[The Fyrehart Clan|the Fyreharts]].
