# The Fyrehart Clan
### Historic Flames Surviving to this Day

## Overview

An extended family descended from the nobility that managed to escape after the fires of [[The Sun|the sun]] claimed the city whose name is no longer remembered by most of the residents.
There are certain ideals that they try to live by, believing that those traits are what made their families fit to be considered nobles.

Through oral tradition, and a few rare books, they have passed down a rough approximation of everything that has led them here today; complete with embellishments, of course.
Hundreds of years ago, their family possessed power, were leaders, controlled armies.
They'd shaped the world, started fashion trends, wrote the laws...
And yet now, here they were, with only some small semblance of duty, and that was only by sheer dumb luck.

If it wasn't for one of their ancestors being the first to discover the storeroom, they would be just another person in this place.
By the grace of [[Gese]] or [[Narys]] or whomever, somehow this ancestor and those with him were able to convince the others that they would be in charge of guarding and securing this most secret of places.
And to this day, that is their role, their task.

Many within the family take it quite seriously, believing that by fulfilling this role, they are living up to the standards of the past.
There are a few who attempt to lord the family's past over others, but this is usually met with confusion and mockery.
From the perspective of everyone else, the members of the Fyrehart Clan are just another family, like any other that had come here.
