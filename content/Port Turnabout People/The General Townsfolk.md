# The General Townsfolk
### Everyone Else

## Members

Tess - A frizzy haired woman in her late forties. She makes candles for the village. Her husband was lost at sea years ago, and she's taken the proper amount of time to grieve, according to her. She's prepared to move on, and seems to be seeking stability. Her eyes have travelled toward the [[The Fieldworkers|fieldworkers]], hoping she might be able to catch the attention of an unexpected target.

Kell, Gertie, and their two children - Kell is a man in his early 30's who's passingly familiar with how to smith things. At this time in the village, that tends to be less useful of a task than the other one he's picked up, fashioning tools out of [[Hardfish]] bones. His wife, Gertie (late 20's), minds many of the village's children while the parents are off doing the day's chores. Because of this task, they've been able to learn a fair amount about the private goings on of [[The Fyrehart Clan]] from their youngest.

Garrick, Molla, and their two children - Garrick and Molla (early 20's, both) have known Yale since they were all kids. The three of them used to travel a good distance away from the village in the summer, when the [[The Sun|sun]] was in the sky the longest. Now, Garrick and Molla still travel in that same way, though it's to fell trees and drag them back before nightfall. One might point out it would be more efficient to set up a base closer to the trees so they could be processed onsite. That's exactly what Garrick's father was convinced to try. He disappeared not long after.
