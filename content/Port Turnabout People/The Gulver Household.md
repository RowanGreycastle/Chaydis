# The Gulver Household
### A Close-Knit Crew

## Overview

A house of the members of smaller families, coming together to make a name for themselves and their own fishing vessel.
The vessel itself, the Gulver, isn't as big as the [[The Riddius Family|Riddius]] or the [[The Phyrus Family|Phyrus]], and isn't as fast as the smaller fishing vessels, but they've been making up for it in quantity of fish.
This is, in part, due to useful information that they were given by Yale of the [[The Militia|Militia]].
