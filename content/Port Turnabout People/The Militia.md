# The Militia
### Kind Words, Helping Hands

## Members

Kale - A skilled swordsman who learned the style of a far off land from a travelling adventurer.
Osco - A spearman who's worked together with Kale since they were kids, and has a personable attitude.
Riley - A spearman who used to get up to all sorts of mischief before being set straight by his dad.
Timmons - A large man who wields a club, speaks softly, and enjoys a good bit of singing.

Yale - A young woman who recently joined the militia, separating herself from the [[The Riddius Family|Riddius Family]] to better help out around the port. She's got a set of skills that has several of the other militiamen anxious. Igo is the one who convinced her to join the militia, and the one who keeps everyone else off her back.

Igo - A young man blessed with the gift of freedom by the god [[Gese]]. He's not initially from here, though after popping out of a hole in the ground that dang near swallowed him up, he wasn't about to try to go back to where he came from. He's found a wife - Tay - and settled into the community proper. Igo believes that members of the [[The Spirit Speaker Clan|Spirit Speakers]] might know things about his gift that he's not yet aware of.
