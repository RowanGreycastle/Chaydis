# The Phyrus Family
### Spear of the Port

## Overview

An extended family in charge of the boat named the Phyrus moored at [[The Docks|the docks]].
The boat holds the right half of the net used to catch [[Hardfish|hardfish]].
The Phyrus is smaller than the [[The Riddius Family|Riddius]], with more oarsmen, allowing it to move about faster and strike.
They take pride in their vessel, keeping it well maintained, so that the hefty fish can't drag them below the waves.

Tagor Phyrus is a prodigy among the family, having been the one to land the decisive blow against three of the heavy fish just this last two years.
He's a tall, muscular man, with straight brown hair, and green eyes.
He's a bit at odds with many of the other families for his personal beliefs, but he's never done anything to put the people of the port into any sort of jeopardy.
The people of the village, from his perspective, should be relying on themselves now that things have settled.
Taking the handouts from unknowable creatures that live beyond the walls was all well and good when there was the risk of starving, but favors need to be repaid.
He feels guilt over the whole thing, believing that they're taking advantage of the [[Spirits|spirits]], while also believing that without them, [[Port Turnabout]] is now stable enough to sustain itself.

The Phyrus household is close to the Southern gate, as they didn't want to have to travel all through town just to be able to maintain things.
The family also helps with the other fishing vessels that are less well manned, of course, in order for those members to learn how to properly do such things.
