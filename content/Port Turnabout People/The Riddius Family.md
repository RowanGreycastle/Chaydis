# The Riddius Family
### Shield of the Port

## Overview

An extended family in charge of the boat named the Riddius moored at [[The Docks|the docks]].
The boat holds the left half of the net used to catch [[Hardfish|hardfish]].
The Riddius is larger and more sturdy than the [[The Phyrus Family|Phyrus]], allowing it to act as the anchor point around which the faster boat can swing.
They take great care in making absolutely sure that their vessel is reinforced, their anchor is solidly affixed, and their attachment points for the net are as firmly on there as they can be.

Nessa Riddius is a stand out family member, not due to any particular skill with fishing, but due to something else entirely.
She was born with the ability to use the mana of sunlight, [[Surahn]].
Thanks to this gift, and encouragement from her family, she is the go-to person in the village for any injuries that the workers might get.
She's an eternal ray of sunshine for nearly everyone; or rather, everyone who doesn't go around thinking they're better than everyone else.

The Riddius household is close to the Southern gate, while Nessa's healer's hut is closer to the center of the village.
The family also assists with the maintenance of the walls, as they're quite familiar with how to make sure things don't break apart.
Years ago, when the [[The Fyrehart Clan|Fyrehart Clan]] made a bid for power, the attempt was quashed by an alliance with the then head of the Riddius Family falling through.
Tensions have been high between the two families ever since.
