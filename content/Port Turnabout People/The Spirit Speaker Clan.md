# The Spirit Speaker Clan
### Guidance From Unseen Hands

## Overview

An extended family in charge of communicating with the [[Spirits|spirits]] surrounding the village, and coming to agreements with them.
They are said to be the descendants of the one who wandered off on his own during one of the darkest days in their history, only to return with an armload of food, and information about where was best to build.
Only a few among their number are actually able to see and discuss with he [[Spirits|spirits]], though everyone who can't strives to be supportive.

Vurdie is one who is gifted with the sight, and one who received much from a wanderer who visited not too long ago.
She is young, but knows the names of the beings that protect the lands beyond the walls.
The influence of the wanderer has recently caused her to become a bit more reclusive and enigmatic; she never goes anywhere without that strange wide-brimmed hat, and she's usually got her head buried in the old book she was given.
The duties of the Spirit Speaker have fallen to her grandmother for the time being.

Grandma Faude is not some sickly old woman, despite appearances to the contrary.
She's still sharp as a tack, witty as anything, and perfectly willing to put her knowledge to use in the way she believes best suits the village.
Especially if that means avoiding the fates of those who came before, and denying position those who see themselves above others.
