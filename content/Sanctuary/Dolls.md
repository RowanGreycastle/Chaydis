# Dolls
### Remotely Operated Adventurers

## Overview

[[Sanctuary]] is surrounded by so much danger that the only way to explore is to do so while not leaving the city.
If not for the ancient technology tucked away within those walls, the inhabitants could very well have long since been deceased.

Dolls can be made of nearly any external material.
They can be soft, sturdy, fragile, rigid, and anything in between.
They require two arms, two legs, a body, and a head for ease of use.
Beyond that point, additional limbs are difficult to control.

The exact mechanism behind their creation and control is only known within the walls of [[Sanctuary]], utterly lost to the rest of the world.
