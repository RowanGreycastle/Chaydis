# Torian Sha
### Underhanded Hero of Sanctuary

## Description

A man in his late 30's with messy brown hair, a bit of a paunch, and an eyepatch.
He carries himself with the swagger of someone utterly unafraid.
Despite his outward shabby appearance, there are glimmers of wealth apparent upon his person.
A patchwork shirt with glinting gold buttons and cufflinks.
Pants held up by a lizard-skin belt.
Two silver loops going through his right ear, each dangling a single large, pointed tooth.

## Personality

Torian is a man who works hard to present himself a certain way.


Currently just typing down ideas for this before bed.
Character is going to be a devious sort who uses dirty tricks as a part of the way he wins fights.
Like all in Sanctuary, he travels outside of the city exclusively through the use of remote controlled dolls.
Unlike the majority, he is quite good at it.
The economic practices of human civilizations past have survived here, after a fashion.
Gambling is popular, due to an ancient rule set down by whichever human civilization first created this city, preventing all forms of violence within its walls.
