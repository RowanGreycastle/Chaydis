# Sunroads
### The Safest Paths

## Overview

Strangely resilient roads have been built into [[Laef]], [[Sten]], and [[Weva]] since a few days after the separation of the great continent.
The roads are made of a multi-layered and intricate pattern of red and silver material that sparkles in the sunlight, the exact composition of which is unknown.
The path of the roads on each continent eventually converge with the others in a 6-spoked hub.
The [[Sunlight Soldiers]] of [[Famott]] patrol these strange roads, making them one of the safest ways to travel during the day.
