# Crescent Cove
### Home of the Best Juice Bar in the Sky

## Overview

A relatively new separation from [[The Moon|the moon]], this crescent shaped hunk of land was caught in an orbit, and now circles the city of [[Lux]].
The glow of the quite close habitat of the [[Acita|acita]] bathes the island in light at all hours of day and night, keeping it quite pleasant for the moths to visit.

A small shack is built close to the center of the island; hanging above the door is a sign, depicting the cross-section of a crescent-shaped fruit overlaid atop the cross-section of a round fruit.
Currently this place is known as The Mellow Crescent Juice Bar, and it's become a hot spot for [[Acita|acita]] to visit for premium service.

Suci, the owner of the place, is a peculiar sort.
Why they opened this bar seems to be entirely for their own amusement, which becomes quite the thing once one discovers the relationships with [[Kalis|kalis]] farmers they had to establish to make this all work, and the amount of trust being put in the [[Varin|varin]] to properly transport it all up.
