# Mt. Thorn
### Rain Source for Laef

High above, travelling amongst the clouds, a large hunk of [[The Moon|the moon]] has broken free.
Unlike so many of the chips that have come off, it did not then stay in place.
Unlike the rest, it also did not follow a pattern in the sky close to [[The Moon|the moon]]'s own.
This strange sky mountain seems to be drawn to [[Laef]], circling above, gathering water from the clouds and raining it down upon the populace.

Could this have been some strange agreement between [[Verath]] and [[Gese]]?
Certainly.
Could it have been random chance?
Possible, but almost certainly not.
Could it have been [[The Moon|the moon]] itself that'd made the decision to give life-granting water to the people of [[Laef]]?
Entirely possible.
Though we may never know the true answer.
