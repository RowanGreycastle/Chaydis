# Port Cinwen
### Trade Hub of the Unclaimed Continent

## Overview

This land was claimed nearly a century and a half ago by the ancestors of the fishermen who live here now.  
Since those humble beginnings, many have moved in gradually; some more welcome than others.  
In current times, the settlement has grown to the size of a trade port, and the import and export of goods for all of the [[Unclaimed Continent]] primarily passes through here.  
A quartet of [[Acita]] moved in a few decades ago, after one of their strange wards worked.  
Even without tall walls and strong defenses, the people of Port Cinwen feel comfortable coming out at night.
