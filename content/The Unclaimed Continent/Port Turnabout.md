# Port Turnabout
### Survivors of the Separation

## Overview

The people who live here now are mainly the descendants of those that migrated here after [[The Sun|the sun]] split the lands.
With nowhere else to go, and food dwindling, they'd opted to call this desolate stony shore their home.
How they made it through is, of course, explained by plenty a local tale.
Nowadays, they survive off of fish, the occasional livestock that they can manage to keep alive, and a strange abundance of fresh produce.
