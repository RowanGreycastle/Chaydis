# The Crossed Seas
### Unwelcome and Sudden

## Overview

A relatively new addition to Chaydis, these seas were created by [[Famott]] to separate the greater landmass of Chaydis into separate [[Continents|continents]].
Their formation was sudden and unexpected, leading to the loss of several human cities.
[[Linmor]] continues to exist, a sealed off city hovering half way between [[Weva]] and [[Sten]].
