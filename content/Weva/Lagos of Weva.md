# Lagos of Weva
### Brutally Honest

## Overview

The islands that dot this section of the world hold just as many unique traditions.
The islands themselves frequently hold secrets that necessitate such things.
All the same, some things hold true to them all, for both counts.

## Naming Traditions

The last name of a [[Lagos|lagos]] is, specifically, the name of their parent.
Whether that parent is biological or not is of no importance.
It is simply a signifier that the one holding it under that namesake's protection.
A [[Lagos|lagos]] under a parent's protection cannot be challenged to a duel.
All challenges must be issued to the parent in their stead.

The first name is a name that they have chosen for themselves once they have come of age.

## Shared Customs and Values

Being able to defend oneself is seen as a necessity.
Demonstration of how to accomplish this is the responsibility of the parent, or one designated by the parent.

Once a challenge has been issued and accepted, any method used to achieve victory (short of including third parties in the fight) is considered honorable.
There are no qualms regarding sneak attacks, distractions, the usage of poisons, or anything of that sort.
Lethal poisons are generally avoided all the same, as that may negatively impact the potential offspring that would come of it.

Collateral damage is viewed as dishonorable, and so combatants are encouraged to take their fighting outside of settlements, or at least to somewhere large enough where buildings won't be damaged.

During hunts and long trips without access to bathing water, [[Lagos|lagos]] use sand and dirt to deaden their scent.

Expressing one's true feelings is seen as standard procedure, no matter the topic.
Even informing someone that they are repulsive is unlikely to be the cause for a fight.

## Art and Architecture

The small huts that are built by the [[Lagos|lagos]] are, as a general rule, propped up on stilts to avoid being swept away or damaged by shifting tides.

## Food and Cuisine

The carnivorous diet of the [[Lagos|lagos]] calls for an ample amount of meat available to them no matter the season.
Various preservation methods have been developed to allow for this ready access, and the various flavors that are brought to the meat by these methods have become sought after for certain dishes.

Some example foods are:
Meat dry aged in the seeds and skin of [[Blackpepper]], which can be stored safely for up to four months.
Meat marinated in an [[Assassin's Root]] sauce for no less than ten days before being seared and served.
Raw meat sliced thinly, served with a dip of [[Yipfrog]] oil.

Each of these foods would be considered deathly poisonous by most other creatures.
To a [[Lagos|lagos]], the additions are fantastic seasonings that bring out the best aspects of the meat, mingling with the scents and flavors already present without overpowering them.

## Common Traditions and Rituals

The act of reproduction is a dance of martial prowess.
Due to the child going to the victor, and the unique circumstances of the offspring's creation, having more children is viewed as a demonstration of one's strength and knowledge in such matters.
As such, being seen without a tail is a clear sign of two things:
A formidable opponent was encountered, and a loss was accrued.

## Coming of Age Practices

Once one has proven themselves to the point where they no longer need their parent's protection, they shed their last name and become their own person.
This is not to say that all relationship with their parent is now in the past, but they are expected to fight their own battles from that point forward.
They may still ask for help if needed, though it is not provided without asking.

## Common Taboos

*Soon there will be nothing left but a series of falsehoods.*"
