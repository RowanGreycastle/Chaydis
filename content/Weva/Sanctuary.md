# Sanctuary
### The City of Dolls

## Overview

_"Upon one of the many isles of [[Weva]], a strange, metallic dome can be found through the devouring mists and sinking bogs._
_Nothing seems to enter or leave, save these oddly twitchy, jerky creatures that look as though they're being led along by unseen strings._
_The majority seem to be made of odds and ends, cloth and wood, scrap metal and leather and bone._
_Each grouping is led by something more skilled, more fluid in its movement; a hunter, or at least one who moves as such, crafted in a far more precise manner._

_The leader of the group guides their followers through a number of strange rituals and routines._
_Once around the perimeter._
_An investigation of the waters surrounding the structure._
_A march to six equidistant points quite a ways away._

_Each outing seems to involve at least one of the less articulate followers falling into the muck._
_Their leader does not halt the ritual to save their comrade; a pup who was not strong enough to survive these lands is lost._

_And once the ritual has concluded, one by one, the remaining creatures file in through the solid wall of the structure, passing inside as though it's not present._
  
_There are still many things in this small corner of Chaydis that are a mystery."_
**- An unnamed lagos**

## Information

The city of Sanctuary is situated at the center of a crater, caused by a long forgotten war.
Due to the shape of the surrounding lands, [[Silent Fog]] surrounds it on all sides, making passing in and out quite the feat for any living creature.
Additionally, the opaque metallic barrier that surrounds the city makes any aerial entry utterly impossible; at least by the standards of current day.

Those within use [[Dolls]] to travel out into the world; their task is to maintain the external facilities of the city, and to search for resources.
Piloting a [[Dolls|Doll]] to accomplish these tasks is considered a civic duty, ensuring that there is always a sizeable force in the event that anything comes along to threaten the populace.
