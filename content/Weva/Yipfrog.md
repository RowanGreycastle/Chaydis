# Yipfrog

## Overview

A small species of frog that can be found on the isles of [[Weva]].
Vibrant yellows and greens cause them to stand out from the rest of the foliage in which they make their home.
They secrete an oil that causes a swelling effect in most creatures, constricting the airway and causing the lungs to spasm, forcing violent hiccups that come out as a 'yip', thus their name.
